Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

MathLock: Math Alarm does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data on your device

Your alarms, settings and progress are saved only on your iPhone. They never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Alarms

Alarms are scheduled on your phone with Apple's AlarmKit. No data is sent to a server.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open a request.
